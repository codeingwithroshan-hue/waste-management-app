WHY IT MATTERS

Separation at Source Powers Circular Cities

When waste is mixed, over 80% ends up polluting soil, air, and water in landfills. Segregating clean plastics, metals, paper, and food scraps at home allows over 90% to be remanufactured back into clean industrial supply chains.

Just two minutes of sorting in your home protects sanitation workers and preserves clean water and air for the whole community.
